Simple Guide — Al'ar

Overall

  • Two elevated platforms; boss flies between them at 80% / 55% / 30% health
  • Swap on Flame Buffet stacks; Kill embers before they reach the raid
  • Raid follows the active platform during Dive Bomb / Rebirth — do not stand in flame patches
  • Hunter MD on pull so MT can position at the edge
Tank InformationExpand
  • MT holds Al'ar on the current platform; OT picks up loose embers if needed
  • Taunt swap every 2–3 Flame Buffet stacks (more stacks = heavy fire damage)
  • Melt Armor on current tank — keep healing steady through armor shred
  • During flight phase, tanks stay on the platform the boss will return to

Ground phase

  • Melee stack behind the boss; spread casters to avoid chain Ember Blast
  • Flame Buffet on MT — call swaps clearly
  • Burn boss; push transitions at 80% / 55% / 30%

Flight & rebirth

  • Boss flies to the far platform and casts Flame Quills — entire raid moves to that platform
  • Dive Bomb marks a player; everyone else spreads, target runs to an empty corner
  • At ~35% boss uses Rebirth at the centre — run to the rebirth platform, kill all embers instantly
  • Embers cast Ember Blast if they reach players — AoE them down before re-engaging boss

Simple Guide — Void Reaver

Overall

  • Straightforward tank-and-spank — positioning and orb awareness win the fight
  • Melee stack directly behind the boss; ranged form a loose circle around the room
  • Arcane Orbs spawn throughout the fight — silent, high damage; watch your feet
  • Hunter MD on pull so the tank can grab the boss at the room edge
Tank InformationExpand
  • Single MT holds Void Reaver still at the pull spot — do not drag the boss through the raid
  • Pounding hits the tank for heavy physical damage every ~15s — anticipate spikes
  • Keep the boss faced away from ranged so cleave never clips the circle

Raid positioning

  • Melee: max melee behind boss, minimal movement except for orbs
  • Ranged: spread along the outer ring with gaps between players
  • Move out of Arcane Orb patches immediately — they do minimal visual/audio telegraph
  • Do not run through the boss to reach an orb; path around the outside

Simple Guide — Solarian

Overall

  • Phase 1 (100–20%): stack and burn; assigned ranged handle Wrath of the Astromancer
  • Transition at 75% / 50% / 25%: Solarian teleports centre, splits into four packs on platforms
  • 20%: Solarian Transform — void form with Void Bolt; collapse DPS when heals are ready
  • Hunter MD on pull; kill Solarian Priest adds quickly during transitions
Tank InformationExpand
  • MT tanks Solarian in P1 and void P3; OTs pick up priest packs on each platform during splits
  • Wrath targets are usually ranged — OT can intercept if a wrath runs toward the stack
  • During Blinding Light (50% transition) raid spreads then re-stacks after the blast

Phase 1 — stack

  • Raid stacks in melee range for maximum healing efficiency
  • Wrath of the Astromancer on two random players — assigned bait run to an empty platform and stand still until it expires
  • Do not let wraths explode in the stack — keep bait players topped
  • Push cleanly through 75% and 50%

Splits & priests

  • At each transition Solarian vanishes; four groups of adds appear on the four platforms
  • Split raid into four teams — kill Arcane Torrent priests first (they mana-burn and heal)
  • Watch for Psychic Scream — fear breaks the pull; tremor / fear ward helps
  • Regroup on boss when adds are dead; 50% transition also has Blinding Light — spread, then stack again

Void phase (20%)

  • Solarian becomes a voidwalker — tank faces her away, raid spreads slightly for Void Bolt
  • Burn with bloodlust / cooldowns once raid is stable
  • Healers: coordinate heroism / potions with raid collapse timing

Simple Guide — Kael'thas

Overall

  • Long encounter: four legendary weapons, then four advisors one at a time, then Kael
  • Guild picks advisor kill order (common: Thaladred → Sanguinar → Capernian → Telonicus)
  • Weapons must be looted and equipped before their advisor phase — tanks need correct resist gear
  • Phase 5 Kael: interrupt Pyroblast, break Mind Control, kill Phoenix eggs fast

Weapons phase

  • Seven weapons spawn — assign one player per weapon to loot and equip immediately
  • Tanks grab their advisor's weapon (e.g. Devastation for Thaladred, Infinity for Capernian)
  • Burn all weapons down before the first advisor activates — do not leave weapons alive into P1

Advisors

  • Thaladred: kite away from raid; Psychic Blow on tank — heavy heals
  • Lord Sanguinar: tank in place; Bellowing Roar fear — tremor totem / fear ward
  • Grand Astromancer Capernian: ranged tank/kite; Conflagration — run out of raid, do not spread dots
  • Master Engineer Telonicus: tank at range; Bomb on random targets — dispel / spread slightly
  • Hunter MD each advisor pull; kill in your guild's order without overlapping active advisors

Kael phase 1 (100–50%)

  • MT picks up Kael; melee behind, casters at max range
  • Shock Barrier — boss immune until barrier drops; then burn
  • Pyroblast on random target — mage must counterspell every cast
  • Flame Strike under random players — move out of fire patches
  • Mind Control on up to three players — kill MC'd players quickly or CC them
  • Phoenix spawns — burn bird, then destroy the egg before rebirth

Kael phase 2 (50%)

  • Kael casts Gravity Lapse — entire raid floats; use slow-fall abilities if available
  • While airborne he summons Nether Vapor — land, spread, and kill vapors before re-engaging
  • Same rules after gravity ends: CS pyro, kill MC, destroy phoenix eggs
  • Heavy tank damage through barrier windows — coordinate cooldowns for final burn
