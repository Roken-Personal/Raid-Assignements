Simple Guide — Hydross

Overall

  • Always starting Frost Form
  • All raid spread out
  • Swaps form based on where the boss is
  • On Swap: Aggro Reset + 4 Adds spawn
  • Bloodlust when threat is good
Tank InformationExpand
  • Each form puts a stacking debuff on you — Frost: Mark of Hydross, and Nature: Mark of Corruption
  • It stacks 5 times for 10%, 25%, 50%, 100% and 250% more damage taken from that element while you're attuned to it
  • Move the boss when damage is too high to force the swap

Frost Form

  • Spread out — it picks one player; anyone within ~8 yards takes damage and is stunned
  • Melee spread around the boss as much as possible

Nature Form

  • Once swapping, pause healing and DPS briefly where possible to prepare for aggro reset and adds
  • Let tanks pick up adds and the boss with help from Misdirect
  • Kill adds, then back on the boss
  • DoT on one target, reducing their damage and healing
