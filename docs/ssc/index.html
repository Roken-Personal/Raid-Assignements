Simple Guide — Hydross

Overall

  • Always starting Frost Form
  • All raid spread out
  • Swaps form based on where the boss is
  • On Swap: Aggro Reset + 4 Adds spawn
  • Bloodlust when threat is good
Tank InformationExpand
  • This boss cannot crush — only need to be crit immune
  • Each form stacks a debuff on you (up to 5 times; escalating damage from that element):
    Mark of Hydross
    Mark of Corruption.
  • Move the boss when damage is too high to force the swap

Frost Form

  • Spread out for Water Tomb
  • Melee spread around the boss as much as possible

Nature Form

  • On swap: ease heals/DPS briefly; tanks pick up boss and adds (Misdirect helps)
  • Kill adds, then back on the boss
  •  Spot heal those affected by Vile Sludge.

Simple Guide — Lurker

Overall

  • Fish the boss up on the platform to start the encounter
  • MT faces Lurker away from the raid; ranged/melee stack in front
  • Spread for Whirl; run behind the boss for Spout
  • At 75% / 50% / 25%: submerge — dodge Geyser and kill Coilfang Guardians from the outer platforms
  • Platform duty: one mage group | one hunter group | one warlock group
  • Hunter MD on pull
Tank InformationExpand
  • Keep the boss anchored; do not spin during Spout
  • Soak Water Bolt; move out when Whirl starts
  • Pick up any guardians that reach the main raid

Surface phase

  • Stay in front of the boss between mechanics
  • On Spout: run through or around the boss so you end up behind it (avoid knockback into the water)
  • Melee: spread for Whirl, then stack back in
  • Top off after whirl and spout windows

Submerge & platforms

  • Boss dives at 75% / 50% / 25%; spread and move out of Geyser
  • Coilfang Guardians spawn on the outer platforms — kill adds on your assigned platform
  • Mage group — assigned platform(s)
  • Hunter group — assigned platform(s)
  • Warlock group — assigned platform(s)
  • Clear guardians before the boss resurfaces, then resume the surface rotation

Simple Guide — Leotheras

Overall

  • Clear the four Greyheart Warlocks channeling green banish beams, then pull the boss
  • Fight alternates humanoid and demon forms until 15%, then both are active at once (final split)
  • Demon transitions at roughly 85% / 70% / 55% / 40% / 25% health
  • Paladin tanks the demon; humanoid MT tanks Leotheras in elf form
  • Hunter MD on pull; hard enrage timer — push damage
Tank InformationExpand
  • Humanoid MT: hold Leotheras during elf phase; move out when Whirlwind starts (melee swap in)
  • Demon tank: pick up the demon form; keep Chaos Blast interrupted (rogue kick / shaman earth shock)
  • Demon MT: righteous fury + consecrate threat; stay in range of your healer
  • Threat resets when the boss swaps forms; coordinate taunts

Humanoid phase

  • Melee leave the boss during Whirlwind; ranged keep DPS from safe distance
  • Heavy damage on the MT during whirl — be ready to top
  • Each dead warlock before pull means one fewer demon spawn during the fight

Demon phase & inner demons

  • Boss becomes a demon for ~30s at each threshold — demon tank gathers, raid focuses boss
  • Interrupt every Chaos Blast on the demon
  • Insidious Whisper on five random players — each spawns an Inner Demon only they can damage
  • Kill your Inner Demon within ~30s or you will be mind controlled
  • Spot-heal players burning down their demon

Final split (15%)

  • Humanoid and demon are both active — do not let either leak
  • Same rules: whirl on humanoid, interrupt chaos blast on demon, kill inner demons immediately
  • Bloodlust / burn once adds and demons are controlled

Simple Guide — Karathress

Overall

  • Four targets: three Fathom-Guards plus Karathress — kill adds before the boss
  • Kill order: Tidalvess → Sharkkis → Caribdis → Karathress
  • Each dead add empowers Karathress; avoid Blessing of the Tides (75% with adds still up)
  • Hunter MD on pull to Tidalvess; kill Spitfire Totem immediately when it spawns
Tank InformationExpand
  • MT holds Karathress after adds; heavy Cataclysmic Bolt on mana users — paladin MTs are risky
  • Spread boss / shaman / priest ~45 yards where possible (volley and bolt range)
  • Off-tanks: Sharkkis + pet, Tidalvess first, Caribdis in a corner group
  • Run out of melee for Sear Nova

Tidalvess (Shaman)

  • Frost Shock on tank — grounding totem helps
  • Drops Spitfire Totem (priority kill), Poison Cleansing Totem, Earthbind Totem
  • Burn totems and add; when dead, boss gains spitfire totems

Sharkkis (Hunter) & Caribdis (Priest)

  • Sharkkis: Multishot, Leeching Throw, pet — separate pet tank; watch The Beast Within
  • Caribdis: interrupt every Healing Wave; dodge Summon Cyclone
  • Water Bolt Volley and Tidal Surge — spread / ready after surge
  • When Caribdis dies, boss gains Tidal Surge

Karathress (boss)

  • Cataclysmic Bolt on random mana users — huge hit, stay topped
  • Sear Nova melee-range fire — tanks and melee move out
  • After Sharkkis dies, boss has The Beast Within (hard-hitting melee)
  • Burn boss once all adds are dead; ignore late spitfire totems if healing through

Simple Guide — Morogrim

Overall

  • Tank Morogrim on the upper platform; raid stays away from the waterfalls (collateral Watery Grave splash)
  • Each Earthquake summons two packs of murlocs — add tank gathers, then AoE down
  • Designated healers on grave victims at the bottom of the stairs
  • At 25%: stops graves, spawns Water Globules — reposition out of range
  • Hunter MD boss to MT on pull; Salvation / Tranquil Air help aggro control
Tank InformationExpand
  • MT: face boss away from raid; heavy burst — expect Tidal Wave frontal frost slow
  • OT: pick up both murloc packs after earthquake; let tanks establish aggro before raid AoE
  • Jump Earthquake to reduce damage; do not heal murlocs until OT has control

Main phase

  • Ranged spread for Watery Grave (four players teleported under falls)
  • Melee behind boss; move out of Tidal Wave cone
  • Top MT after earthquake + graves before burning murlocs

Murloc waves

  • After every Earthquake: 12 murlocs total — OT tanks at stair bottom, raid AoE from platform edge
  • Do not heal too early or murlocs will chase healers
  • Seed / AoE once OT has solid threat

Globules (25%)

  • Boss summons four Water Globules from grave spots — they walk to targets and explode
  • Raid moves into the doorway toward the next room to avoid contact
  • Resume DPS on boss; watch for Tidal Wave while kiting globules

Simple Guide — Vashj

Overall

  • Phase 1 (100–70%): tank-and-spank with spread for Static Charge
  • Phase 2 (70–50%): Vashj immune behind Magic Barrier — kill adds, loot s, deactivate four shield generators
  • Phase 3 (50%): same as P1 plus Toxic Spores bats (soft enrage)
  • Hunter MD on pull; grounding totem on MT for Shock Blast
Tank InformationExpand
  • MT P1/P3: grounding for Shock Blast; Freedom on Entangle
  • Static Charge on MT — melee stay clear; victim moves to middle
  • P2: tanks on Coilfang Elites in inner circle; strider kited wide outside generators
  • Clear remaining P2 adds before burning boss in P3

Phase 1

  • Spread raid to minimize Static Charge chain damage
  • Multi-Shot and shoot hit random targets — spot heal
  • Shock Blast on MT — no threat drop; keep healing through
  • Push to 70% then reset positions for phase 2

Phase 2 — shield & cores

  • Vashj immune at centre; Forked Lightning from platform — stay spread on stairs
  • Kill Enchanted Elementals before they reach her (+5% damage each)
  • Tainted Elemental every ~50s: kill fast, loot , use on a shield generator
  • Coilfang Elite: tank + melee burn; Strider: kite with slows/roots + DoTs
  • Poison Bolt from tainted adds — dispel / heal
  • Time last generator so minimal adds remain entering P3

Phase 3

  • Aggro reset — MD boss to MT; kill leftover striders/elites first
  • Toxic Spores bats spawn increasing poison pools — assign hunters/warlocks to clear early bats
  • Same as P1: spread, grounding, Freedom; burn boss before poison overwhelms raid
